# Sri Lanka Payroll Gap Analysis

| Module/Feature | Current status (Present/ Partial/ Missing) | Why Sri Lanka needs it | Recommendation/Design | Priority (P0/P1/P2) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recurring Allowances & Deductions engine | Partial – catalog entities exist but loading recurring lines into payslips is TODO.| Required to automatically apply fixed and formula-based allowances/deductions (e.g., transport, mobile, loans) each pay period without manual entry.| Deliver a recurring engine that (1) provides API endpoints to create/update recurrence rules (frequency, start/stop, eligibility, proration, EPF/ETF/tax flags) and to simulate next-run amounts, (2) injects recurring lines into payslips during pay-run generation with idempotency and retro adjustments, and (3) adds web UI for HR to manage recurring allowances/deductions and preview their impact before approval.| P0 |
| PayRun lifecycle & approvals | Partial – status flag only; no workflow or approver tracking.| Compliance requires maker-checker approval trail before locking payroll for payment.| Add approval workflow with stages (draft → prepared → approved → locked), approver assignments, timestamps, and comments; enforce state transitions and permissions.| P0 |
| Audit log store | Missing – only entity-level timestamps exist, no central log.| Auditability for statutory inspections and internal controls.| Introduce append-only audit log capturing user actions (create/update pay-runs, payslip adjustments, approvals) with before/after values; expose search/export API.| P0 |
| Statutory EPF/ETF config & reporting | Partial – EPF/ETF rule sets exist and calculations occur, but reporting/export not implemented.| Employers must file monthly EPF/ETF schedules (C, R3) and reconcile contributions.| Build report generator producing EPF/ETF summaries per pay-run/month with CSV/PDF exports; validation against thresholds; mapping of earning lines to contributable base.| P1 |
| PAYE/APIT tax engine | Present – tax slabs and computation applied; reporting/export missing.| Monthly APIT schedules and certificates required for IRD filing.| Extend tax engine with APIT monthly/annual reports, certificate generation, and support for reliefs/rebates as per Sri Lanka rules.| P1 |
| Leave integration into payroll math | Missing – leave records stored but not applied to earnings/deductions.| Need accurate no-pay/encashment based on approved leave and balance rules.| Integrate LeaveRequest data into pay-run: calculate no-pay, leave encashment, and partial-day handling; reconcile with attendance to avoid double deductions.| P0 |
| Overtime rule config | Partial – OT records loaded; no rule configuration beyond raw hours and type.| Sri Lanka payroll often uses tiered OT rates (1.5x/2x) and caps by shift/holiday.| Add OT rule engine with rate multipliers by type/weekday/holiday, rounding rules, and auto-calculation of earnings from hours; lock approved OT for payroll.| P1 |
| Bank export files (HNB/BOC/Commercial templates) | Missing – no banking/export integration.| Banks require specific bulk salary upload formats to release payments.| Implement bank-specific export templates (CSV/TXT) with account validation, net pay amounts, and separate files per pay-run; track export status.| P0 |
| Payslip PDF export | Partial – payslip data accessible; no PDF generation/export.| Employees need printable payslips compliant with statutory format.| Generate PDF/HTML payslips with earning/deduction breakdown, statutory amounts, and digital signature; enable email/download.| P1 |
| General Ledger (Accounting posting) | Missing – no accounting postings from payroll.| Finance needs payroll journals for ERP; compliance with audit and reconciliation.| Add GL mapping of earnings/deductions to accounts, produce journal entries per pay-run (debit/credit), and export to accounting systems.| P1 |
| Multi company/Branch/Cost center | Missing – no multi-entity segmentation.| Larger employers segment payroll by company/branch/cost center for reporting and approvals.| Introduce organizational hierarchy on employees and pay-runs; scope rules and reports by entity, enable consolidated vs. per-branch outputs, and segregated approvals.| P0 |
